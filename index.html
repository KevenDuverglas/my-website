About Me

I'm Keven Michel Duverglas, an award-winning 3D artist with a deep passion for visual storytelling and cinematic design. I grew up in Haiti, where I spent over 17 years immersed in a culture that shaped my creative vision and drive. For the past 5 years, I've been creating artwork in Blender — from hard-surface modeling to full scene compositions and animated shorts. My work blends imagination with emotion, bringing digital worlds to life with attention to detail and powerful themes.
